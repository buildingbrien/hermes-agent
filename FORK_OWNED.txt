0034-vision-honest-results-and-screenshot-names.patch — tools/vision_tools.py: vision_analyze sends images over ~1 MB downscaled as JPEG under ~1.4 MB base64 with the long edge at most 2048 px (upstream's resizer halves, so a 2880 px Retina screenshot goes out at 1440 px), retries once from the original under ~700 KB with the long edge at most 1568 px (usually the same size at a lower JPEG quality) when the model answers that it could not see the picture, and returns success:false after a second unseen answer or an empty one, instead of success:true (F50). "Could not see" is deliberately narrow and anchored at the start of the answer: empty, the bare "[Unsupported Image]" placeholder, the input itself coming through as that placeholder ('The image appears as "[Unsupported Image]"'), a refusal whose object is the whole input ("I can't see the image you attached."), or "No image was provided/attached" about the request (those two must end the sentence or go on to say why: "There is no image in the message; the email body is text" and 'The image appears as "[Unsupported Image]" in the Gmail preview pane' describe a screen), or the placeholder together with a first-person "I can't see/view it" in the first two sentences of a short answer. An answer about what is on screen ("I don't see a photo in the profile header; it shows the initials BC", "the hero image did not load") is a real answer: image-presence and upload checks depend on it. tools/image_source.py: a local path that does not exist byte-for-byte resolves to the ONE unicode-equivalent name in its folder (macOS screenshot names carry U+202F before AM/PM), then goes through the same host-read and credential checks; several candidates resolve to none. Test: runtime-addons/tests/tools/test_lucaryin_vision_honesty.py.
